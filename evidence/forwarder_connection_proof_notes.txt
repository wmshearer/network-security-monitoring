Forwarder connection proof, splunk-ingest-pipeline
====================================================

Search (run on the indexer, https://localhost:8089):
  index=_internal sourcetype=splunkd source=*metrics.log group=tcpin_connections
  | stats count values(fwdType) values(connectionType) values(destPort) values(guid) by hostname

Result (see forwarder_connection_proof.json for raw output):
  hostname=kali  count=24  fwdType=uf  connectionType=cooked  destPort=9997
  guid=980FCE87-A95B-45BE-976F-A07F972447FA

This is the indexer's OWN record of an inbound TCP connection on its
receiving port 9997, explicitly tagged fwdType=uf (Universal Forwarder,
distinct from an indexer-to-indexer or HF connection) and
connectionType=cooked (the s2s forwarder protocol, not a local file input or
raw syslog).

Cross-check: the UF's own instance.cfg
(/home/kali/splunkforwarder/etc/instance.cfg) reports:
  guid = 980FCE87-A95B-45BE-976F-A07F972447FA
(copied to uf_instance_cfg.txt in this evidence/ directory)

The GUID recorded by the indexer as the source of the inbound cooked
connection is IDENTICAL to the UF's own instance GUID. This is the
distinguishing proof asked for in the task brief: both instances run on the
same physical host (host field is "kali" on both sides, since there's no
separate hostname to distinguish them), so host/splunk_server alone would
not prove forwarding took place. The GUID match does: it identifies the
specific Splunk instance (the UF) that opened the connection, independent of
hostname collision.
